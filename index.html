Welcome to Chenyao Bathrooms. Our factory is located in Shangqiu, Henan Province, China, specializing in wall-mounted and freestanding bathroom cabinets. We are experts in manufacturing solid-core plywood, paint-free bathroom cabinets. Equipped with modern facilities and imported intelligent machinery, we ensure standardized mass production, delivering consistent, efficient, and high-quality products.

We offer comprehensive one-stop manufacturing solutions for importers, wholesalers, and distributors—whether you need OEM production under your brand, custom-sized units for the U.S./EU markets, or large-scale project deliveries, we’ve got you covered.
